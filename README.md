# 📊 Customer Shopping Behaviour Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

A leading retail company wants to better understand its customers' shopping behavior in order to improve sales, customer satisfaction, and long-term loyalty.

This project analyzes **3,900 customer purchases across various product categories** to uncover insights into spending patterns, customer segments, product preferences, discounts, shipping choices, and subscription behavior. The analysis uses Python, PostgreSQL, and Power BI to turn transactional data into actionable business insights.

### Business Question

> **How can the company leverage consumer shopping data to identify trends, improve customer engagement, and optimize marketing and product strategies?**

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Understand customer purchasing and spending patterns
- Analyze customer segments based on purchase history
- Compare purchasing behavior across demographics
- Identify top-performing products and categories
- Analyze the impact of discounts on purchasing behavior
- Compare subscribers and non-subscribers
- Understand the relationship between repeat purchases and subscriptions
- Analyze revenue contribution by age group
- Compare shipping preferences and purchase amounts
- Build an interactive Power BI dashboard
- Generate data-driven business recommendations

---

## 📂 Dataset

The dataset contains **3,900 rows and 18 columns**.

### Key Features

- **Customer Demographics:** Age, Gender, Location, Subscription Status
- **Purchase Details:** Item Purchased, Category, Purchase Amount, Season, Size, Color
- **Shopping Behavior:** Discount Applied, Promo Code Used, Previous Purchases, Frequency of Purchases, Review Rating, Shipping Type

### Data Quality

- **Missing Values:** 37 values were found in the `Review Rating` column.
- Missing review ratings were imputed using the **median rating of each product category**.

# 🔄 Project Workflow

## 1. Data Loading & Initial Exploration

The dataset was imported into Python using Pandas.

Initial exploration was performed using:

- `df.info()` to understand the dataset structure
- `df.describe()` for summary statistics
- Null-value checks
- Duplicate checks
- Data-type validation

---

## 2. Data Cleaning

The following data-cleaning activities were performed:

- Checked for missing values
- Imputed missing `Review Rating` values using the median rating of each product category
- Renamed columns using **snake_case** for consistency and readability
- Checked data consistency between `discount_applied` and `promo_code_used`
- Removed the redundant `promo_code_used` column

---

## 3. Feature Engineering

New features were created to support deeper analysis:

### `age_group`

Customer ages were grouped into meaningful age categories.

### `purchase_frequency_days`

A purchase-frequency feature was created from the purchase data to support customer behavior analysis.

---

## 4. PostgreSQL Database Integration

The cleaned Pandas DataFrame was connected to **PostgreSQL** and loaded into the database for structured SQL analysis.

This enabled business-focused queries to analyze customer behavior, revenue, products, subscriptions, discounts, and purchasing patterns.

---

# 🗄️ SQL Business Analysis

The following business questions were answered using PostgreSQL:

### 1. Revenue by Gender

Compared total revenue generated by male and female customers.

### 2. High-Spending Discount Users

Identified customers who used discounts but still spent above the average purchase amount.

### 3. Top 5 Products by Rating

Identified products with the highest average review ratings.

### 4. Shipping Type Comparison

Compared average purchase amounts between **Standard** and **Express** shipping.

### 5. Subscribers vs. Non-Subscribers

Compared average spending and total revenue between subscribers and non-subscribers.

### 6. Discount-Dependent Products

Identified the **5 products with the highest percentage of discounted purchases**.

### 7. Customer Segmentation

Classified customers into:

- **New**
- **Returning**
- **Loyal**

based on their purchase history.

### 8. Top 3 Products per Category

Identified the three most purchased products within each category.

### 9. Repeat Buyers & Subscriptions

Analyzed whether customers with **more than 5 purchases** are more likely to subscribe.

### 10. Revenue by Age Group

Calculated the total revenue contribution of each customer age group.

Evaluate the effectiveness of discounts while balancing increased sales with margin control.

Highlight top-rated and best-selling products in marketing campaigns and promotional activities.

Focus marketing efforts on high-revenue age groups and customers who prefer express shipping.
